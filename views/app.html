This app runs on the Koinos Network — every answer is generated on someone else's machine and paid for in KAI. To use it, authorise a spending limit from a wallet you control.

How this works

In the Koinos AI desktop app, open Settings → Koinos AI account and authorise web access. You sign once, on the machine that holds your wallet, to set a cap and an expiry. Nothing here ever holds your key, and this site can never spend more than the cap you set. You can end it at any moment, from either side.

Two separate things

The grant is permission — how much this website may draw. It is not funds. Your wallet also needs KAI on the network to draw against, the same as the desktop app: add some in the Earn tab, or run a node and earn it. Everyone gets a small free allowance each day too.
