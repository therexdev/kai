# Email, calendar, memory

All three live in **Tools & accounts** (the **Tools** sidebar item). All three are off in Local-Only privacy mode, and the panel says so instead of half-working.

## Email — step by step

1. Open **Tools** and scroll to **Email**.
2. Enter your IMAP and SMTP servers, address, and password.
   - **Gmail**: create an [app password](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/185833) (regular passwords won't work), server `imap.gmail.com` / `smtp.gmail.com`.
3. Connect. **You should see** your inbox list appear.

What you get: the AI summarizes threads, drafts replies, finds messages. **Sending is always manual** — the AI writes, you press send. Credentials are encrypted with your OS keychain where available.

## Calendar

1. **Tools → Calendar**, enter your CalDAV server details.
2. Upcoming events appear; ask the AI about your week in chat.
3. The AI can create events — each one shows a confirmation before it lands in your calendar.

## Memory

Covered in depth on the Tools page: local-only storage, pin with 📌, add or delete entries any time, auto-injected only when relevant.
